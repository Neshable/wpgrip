# Managing Subscriptions

All subscription management is available from the **Billing** section of your dashboard.

## Viewing Your Subscription

The billing page shows:

- Current plan and billing cycle
- Next billing date and amount
- Payment method on file
- Subscription status (active, trialing, past due, cancelled)

## Updating Payment Method

Click **Update Payment Method** on the billing page. You’ll be redirected to LemonSqueezy’s secure portal to update your card or payment details.

## Cancelling

1. Go to **Billing**
2. Click **Cancel Subscription**
3. Confirm the cancellation

After cancelling:
- Your subscription remains active until the end of the current billing period
- You keep access to all features until that date
- No further charges will be made
- Your data is retained for 30 days after expiry, then deleted

## Reactivating

If you cancelled but your billing period hasn’t ended yet, you can reactivate from the billing page. If your subscription has expired, you’ll need to start a new subscription.

## Invoices

Invoices are generated automatically for each payment. Download them from the **Billing** page.

## Discounts

If you have a discount code, apply it during checkout. Active discounts are shown on your billing page.
