## Billing Cycles

All plans are available in **monthly** and **yearly** billing cycles. Yearly plans include a discount.

## Changing Plans

You can upgrade or downgrade at any time:

1. Go to **Billing** in the sidebar
2. Click **Change Plan**
3. Select your new plan

**Upgrades** are prorated — you only pay the difference for the remaining billing period.

**Downgrades** take effect at the end of your current billing period.

::: warning
If you downgrade to a plan with lower limits and currently exceed those limits, you won’t lose any existing sites or backups. However, you won’t be able to add new ones until you’re within the new plan’s limits.
:::
